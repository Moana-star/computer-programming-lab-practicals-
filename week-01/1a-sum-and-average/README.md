# 1(a) — Sum and average of three numbers

**Manual:** Week 01, 1(a). These are the exact three samples used in our earlier worked answer.

## Sample 1
**Source file:** `sample-1.c`
**Input:** `2 4 5`
**Calculation:**
- Sum = 2 + 4 + 5 = 11
- Average = 11 / 3 = 3.67
**Expected output:**
```text
Enter three integers: 2 4 5
Sum = 11
Average = 3.67
```
